# 11 — Business and Monetization

## Product business thesis

Efficient Machine is well suited to hybrid monetization because:
- core sessions can be short;
- players can return to persistent progress;
- optional boosts have clear context;
- cosmetics can be generated cheaply;
- deep players may value convenience/blueprint features.

Monetization comes **after** retention proof.

## Free experience promise

A free player must be able to:
- play indefinitely;
- complete all core mechanics;
- unlock the design space through play;
- never be forced to watch an ad to continue a normal machine.

## Rewarded ad candidates

Later only:
- double capped offline reward;
- reroll one contract;
- temporary non-ranked overclock;
- optional hint/analysis;
- temporary test bench slot.

Never show rewarded offers so frequently that optimal play becomes “watch ads”.

## IAP candidates

- Remove ads.
- Cosmetic machine themes.
- Particle/trail styles.
- Additional blueprint organization/slots (ensure free baseline is sufficient).
- Optional supporter pack.

Potential long-term pass only after live-content system exists.

## What not to sell

Avoid direct permanent pay-to-win multipliers that invalidate optimization leaderboards.

Competitive/daily modes must normalize or clearly separate paid advantages.

## Revenue strategy by stage

### Stage 1 — prototype
Revenue = 0. Validate fun.

### Stage 2 — closed/open test
Rewarded ads may be stubbed but not prioritized. Validate retention and contract pacing.

### Stage 3 — soft launch
Test:
- rewarded offline boost;
- remove ads;
- cosmetic/supporter purchase.

### Stage 4 — scale
Only add deeper monetization if cohorts retain and the community does not perceive the game as an ad funnel.

## Cost structure

The design is intentionally cheap to operate:
- offline-first core;
- no server required for MVP;
- procedural visuals;
- no large art pipeline;
- backend added only for leaderboards/cloud sync/live events.

This improves the chance that a small audience can still be economically sustainable.
