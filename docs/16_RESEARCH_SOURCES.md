# 16 — Research and Sources

This file records research that influenced the direction. It is not a claim that any source owns the general idea of optimization/factory games.

## Community inspiration

The original concept direction was inspired by a public community idea describing a grid-based efficiency machine with sources, targets, reflectors/splitters/multipliers and the possibility of completed machines becoming parts of larger machines:

- Reddit / r/gameideas: https://www.reddit.com/r/gameideas/comments/1ac0ef7/

We should not copy unique text, branding, art, level designs or proprietary implementation. The commercial product must develop its own rules, identity, UX, balance and code.

## Market direction reviewed during concept work

Research around 2026 mobile trends indicated continuing strength in puzzle/casual and movement from disposable hyper-casual products toward longer-lived hybrid-casual systems. These references are directional, not guarantees of product success.

Decision implication:
- optimize for immediate readability;
- add long-term progression only around a proven core;
- combine rewarded ads/IAP carefully later rather than designing an ad-first game.
