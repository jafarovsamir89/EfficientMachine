# 15 — Play Store Launch Plan

## Positioning

Do not market as a “factory simulator” first; that suggests complexity. Candidate positioning:

- “Build the smallest machine that makes the biggest number.”
- “One grid. A few modules. Infinite ways to optimize.”
- “Can you beat this output with less power?”

## Store creative direction

All creatives can be generated from gameplay:
- before: `82/s`;
- player moves one module;
- machine reroutes;
- after: `214/s`;
- short payoff text.

This is ideal for low-cost short-form video because the transformation itself is the ad.

## Screenshot concepts

1. Clean early machine — “BUILD”.
2. Dense branching layout — “OPTIMIZE”.
3. Huge output improvement — “MAKE IT FASTER”.
4. Contract constraint — “1000/s UNDER 60 POWER”.
5. Blueprint comparison — “YOUR DESIGN”.

No fake gameplay in store assets.

## Testing ladder

1. Internal devices.
2. Small closed test with observational feedback.
3. Larger closed/open testing focused on retention and confusion points.
4. Country-limited soft launch if needed.
5. Production only after crash/performance/retention baselines are acceptable.

## Policy posture

- minimal permissions;
- offline-first;
- no sensitive permissions needed for core game;
- clear privacy policy before analytics/ads are enabled;
- age rating determined by final social/monetization features.
